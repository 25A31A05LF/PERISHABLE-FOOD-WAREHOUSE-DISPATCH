# Objectives

## Main Objective

To develop a Java-based Perishable Food Warehouse Dispatch System using a Min-Heap data structure to manage food batches according to their expiry dates and follow the First Expiry, First Out (FEFO) principle.

## Specific Objectives

1. **Efficient Inventory Management:** To maintain food batch details, including Batch ID, food name, quantity, and expiry date.

2. **Min-Heap Implementation:** To implement a custom Min-Heap that prioritises batches with the earliest expiry dates.

3. **Batch Insertion:** To insert new food batches while maintaining the Min-Heap property.

4. **Earliest Expiry Identification:** To retrieve the batch with the earliest expiry date efficiently.

5. **Food Dispatch:** To dispatch the required food quantity according to the FEFO principle.

6. **Expiry Date Update:** To update the shelf life of an existing batch and restore the Min-Heap property.

7. **Expired Batch Removal:** To identify and remove expired food batches from the warehouse.

8. **Dispatch Simulation:** To simulate a dispatch day and calculate the total dispatched and wasted food quantities.

9. **Near-Expiry Alerts:** To identify batches that are approaching their expiry dates.

10. **Inventory Reports:** To generate reports containing dispatched quantities, wasted quantities, and remaining inventory.

11. **File Handling:** To save warehouse inventory details in a text file and load previously saved records when the application starts.

12. **Performance Analysis:** To understand and demonstrate the efficiency of Min-Heap operations in managing warehouse inventory.
